# Announcement

120–200 words. One idea. Problem in the customer's words → what they can now do → one concrete flow → where to learn more. No superlatives without a number from the spec; no roadmap.

## Structure

**Subject / headline:** <customer-facing name>, phrased as the benefit (≤ 8 words)

**Paragraph 1 — the problem (PAD §1.1).** One or two sentences a customer would nod at.

**Paragraph 2 — what's new (PAD §2.4 line + spec §5).** What you can now do, in two or three sentences. One concrete flow: "Save your filters once, mark one as default, and the Results page opens to it."

**Paragraph 3 — how to get it.** Availability (plan, rollout stage, flag timing), one link to the help article. `[TBD]` for anything unconfirmed.

**Sign-off** per house style.

## Variants

- **Email:** structure above, plus preheader = paragraph 1's first sentence.
- **In-app:** headline + paragraph 2's first sentence + one button to the help article. ≤ 40 words.
- **Blog / changelog post:** structure above, plus one `[Screenshot: <state>, AC-x.y]` and, if the PAD recorded a baseline, one honest number ("operators told us this took about 40 seconds every session").

## Don'ts

- Don't announce behaviors the validation report says were deferred.
- Don't name internal things (PAD ids, flag names, AC numbers) in customer copy.
- Don't promise the non-goals.
